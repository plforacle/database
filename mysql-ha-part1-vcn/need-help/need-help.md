# Get Help with the Workshop

## Introduction

Use the lab checkpoints to identify the first step that differs from your Console results.

Estimated Time: 5 minutes, optional

### Objectives

- Collect the information needed to investigate a blocked step.

## Task 1: Collect the relevant details

1. Record the lab title, task number, selected region, and error message. Include the resource type and operation that failed.

2. Ask your workshop facilitator or tenancy administrator about permissions, service limits, and account-specific Console behavior. Share resource identifiers only through your organization's approved support channel.

3. For product behavior, consult the Oracle documentation linked under **Learn More** in the affected lab.
